## Sales Analytics Project

## 1. Project Overview
Analysed sales data to evaluate business performance, identify top-performing products and understand revenue and profit trends.

## 2. Dataset
•	Number of rows: 150
•	Number of columns: 17
•	Contains order, customer, product, sales, payment and salesperson information.
•	Dataset type: Practice dataset.

## 3. Tools Used
•	Microsoft Excel
•	Excel Formulas
•	PivotTables
•	Charts
•	Slicers

## 4. Analysis Performed
•	Data cleaning and validation.
•	Revenue, cost and profit calculations.
•	Product and category performance analysis.
•	Regional profit analysis.
•	Salesperson performance analysis.
•	Payment method analysis.
•	Discount and profit relationship investigation.
•	Product return-rate analysis.
•	Monthly sales trends.

## 5. Key Findings & Dashboard
•	Total Revenue: 11445825PKR
•	Total Profit: 2372525PKR
•	Total Orders: 150
•	Average Order Value: 76,305.5
•	Highest-revenue products: Laptop
•	Highest-revenue product category: Electronics
•	Highest-profit region: North
•	Overall Profit Margin: 20.73%
